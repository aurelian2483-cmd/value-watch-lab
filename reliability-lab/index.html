Method and limitations

The historical gate requires one team to have won at least four of its previous five competition matches while the opponent won no more than one. Both sides need five completed prior results.

The 50/50 benchmark uses 50% smoothed recent-form probability and 50% bookmaker probability with margin removed. The challenger derives probability from the same smoothed recent-form proxy without blending bookmaker probability, but only considers the direction favoured by the history gate. Both apply at least 5% estimated expected return and maximum decimal odds 5.00.

UNVALIDATED — research only. This sheet uses a clearly documented illustrative recent-form probability proxy, not the deployed frozen vector or a trained, calibrated historical model. One season of high hit rates does not establish profitable value. The competition selector labels the research context; it does not apply league-specific adjustments. No live site selection logic is changed.
