   Verify the downloaded ZIP against it, then extract into a new folder.

   npx --yes wrangler@4.165.0 login --use-keyring

   node preflight.mjs <EXPECTED_ACCOUNT_ID>

   checks every package digest and runs Wrangler's dry-run bundle build.
   The dry-run bundle is temporary. After success, the script writes a
   non-secret wrangler.account.json pinned to the verified account. It
   never deploys.

   verification fails, STOP. Do not create another account or Worker.

   npx --yes wrangler@4.165.0 deploy --config wrangler.account.json
   Review any prompt. Stop if Wrangler proposes deleting another resource,
   migrating existing data, a paid plan, or a different Worker/account.
