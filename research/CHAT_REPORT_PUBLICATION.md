# Published chat research

The root dashboard reads `master-research-report.json` through `chat-research.js`. The automatic football and horse pipelines update their own model feeds; they do not read ChatGPT conversations or publish new chat messages.

## Publishing a report

1. Recover exact selections from the supplied report, screenshot or available conversation evidence. Do not invent missing legs, dates, supporting statistics or citations.
2. Preserve the previous dated report under `research-reports/` before replacing the latest report. Give the new report a stable report ID, original report date and actual publication time.
3. Include every recovered single and builder from that report. Record incomplete coverage explicitly. Never silently discard entries through a fixed three-item display limit.
4. Match fixture identity and kickoff against the appropriate current research feed. Preserve any corrected home/away order in the provenance notes. A schedule match does not verify supporting claims or qualify a selection.
5. Keep reported observations separate from independently reverified evidence. For unqualified builders, combined price, joint probability and EV remain null. Do not multiply marginal probabilities for correlated legs.
6. Publish JSON and renderer changes together, check the page, and confirm deployment before reporting success. New chat content is not on the website until this publication step succeeds.
