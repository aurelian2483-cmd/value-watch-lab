**PROPOSED · NOT APPROVED.** This is separate from the 608-match
retrospective replay. Keep all three fixed paths in parallel and retain
their existing denominators, clamp order and configuration identities.
Use no market, squad, player or historical forecast substitution.

- **Roster and scope.** Fix one Premier League UTC schedule window before
  the first capture. Document exact provider fixture IDs, declared season,
  ordered teams, earliest independently reviewed kickoff and all revisions.
  Preserve the complete roster, including missing or unready fixtures.
- **Inputs and capture.** Require the Phase 51 shared history, league/form/
  venue gates and exact earlier-result bytes with receipts before cutoff.
  Exclude all same-day results. Require schedule and input evidence, packet
  freeze and independently verified timestamp strictly before the earliest
  verified kickoff. **Propose** at least five minutes of buffer, matching
  the archived expanded producer's existing lead-time guard. Choose the
  study cutoff before intake. No retrospective repair or substitution.
- **Corrections.** Keep original packets, bytes and timestamp records.
  Append supersessions or exclusion reasons. A reschedule or identity
  conflict withholds the original packet. Never replace a missed forecast
  with a late one.
- **Outcomes and scoring.** Review exact regulation-time result sets from
  two independently acquired approved sources after the target fixture.
  Preserve disputes and nonfinal or void outcomes. Close at a fixed UTC
  review deadline after the roster window. Report the same adjudicated
  cohort across all paths, using natural log loss with the 1e-15 floor and
  unscaled multiclass Brier. Report coverage and unresolved rows first.
- **Proposed sufficiency floor.** At least 100 common adjudicated fixtures,
  80% common pre-kickoff capture coverage across the preregistered roster,
  and 90% resolution of captures by the review deadline. These are basic
  operational completeness targets to avoid describing a handful of
  conveniently matched fixtures. They are not a power calculation or a
  validation threshold. Approve or replace them before enrollment. Report
  coverage only if the targets or integrity requirements fail.
- **Stop conditions.** Pause on missing provenance, timestamp verification,
  permission changes, broken archive integrity, incomplete model lineage
  or an unresolvable identity issue. Do not extend windows or modify rules
  after viewing scores. No model selection, publication or production use.
