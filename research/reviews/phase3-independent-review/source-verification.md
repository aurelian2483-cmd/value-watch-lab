# Racing data source verification for external review

Public documentation reviewed 26 November 2026. This review did not use an account, purchase or download a dataset, collect a live result, or put publisher bodies in the repository. Admission remains pending for all candidates.

| Source | Access and retention | Independence, clocks, completeness and revision evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| British Horseracing Authority (BHA) and the official return | The public results search is available. The website terms allow personal extracts, preserve intellectual-property rights and restrict commercial reuse and commercial automated extraction. Permission for the *complete* retained evidence required by Phase 3 is unverified. [Results](https://www.britishhorseracing.com/racing/results/) · [Website terms](https://www.britishhorseracing.com/terms-conditions/) | The BHA says the official return reaches Weatherbys and the stewards, with PA distribution to off-course media. This establishes a common upstream source. Public fields for exact publication, amended-at and off times, all runners and complete revision history were not verified. Official results, regulatory disciplinary outcomes and bookmaker settlement require separate identifiers and timestamps. [Official full result distribution](https://www.britishhorseracing.com/faqs/how-is-the-official-full-race-result-distributed/) · [Rules compliance](https://www.britishhorseracing.com/regulation/rules-compliance/) |
| Racecourse Media Group and PA Betting Services | RMG offers licensed race-day data delivered by PA, including withdrawals, race status, shows and results for its 37 listed racecourses. Written coverage and retention rights for private immutable bodies, an external reviewer and derived exports remain unverified. [RMG race-day data](https://racecoursemediagroup.com/structure-area/race-day-data/) | Different vendor names can still share the official return or PA delivery. Ask for exact licensed venues and historical dates, race/runner key namespaces, explicitly zoned scheduled and actual off times, publication and correction sequence, tombstones, and outage/backfill notices. None was sample-verified here. |
| Irish Horseracing Regulatory Board (IHRB) | Stewards reports, non-runner notices and referrals can be consulted publicly. Permission for automatic bulk retrieval and retention of complete result bodies for republication or external review is unverified. [IHRB raceday resources](https://www.ihrb.ie/Raceday) | Useful as a regulatory adjudication cross-check within the documented scope. The public pages did not establish a complete licensed results feed, immutable revision history or clock guarantees. Regulatory review and source independence must be assessed per race. |
| The Racing API | Authenticated products expose race and horse IDs, racecards and results according to entitlement. The service prohibits unauthorised resale, and the public terms do not establish the proposed raw-body external reviewer or repository retention rights. Historical reach is plan-dependent. [API documentation](https://api.theracingapi.com/documentation) · [Terms](https://www.theracingapi.com/terms-of-service) | The provider describes itself as unofficial and uses aggregated data and manual input. Independence from the official return, complete runner and abandoned-race coverage, exact first availability and append-only amendment exports remain unverified. A different namespace can assist reconciliation after a licensed sample verifies the join. |
| Sporting Life | Its website terms constrain automated acquisition and reuse. No permission to retain and redistribute complete result bodies was verified. [Website terms](https://www.sportinglife.com/terms-and-conditions) | Public results remain a manual corroboration candidate. A hostname, display timestamp or distinct rendering alone cannot establish upstream independence, finality or completeness. |
| Betfair Exchange Historical Data | Registered customer access and package entitlement apply. Published historical terms restrict use to personal/internal purposes and limit onward distribution without written agreement. No paid package was acquired. [Historical terms and feed specification](https://historicdata.betfair.com/Betfair-Historical-Data-Feed-Specification.pdf) | The stream schema distinguishes event/market identity, selection identity, market state and the stream publication time in epoch milliseconds. Basic is one-minute traded-price data, Advanced carries one-second best offers, and Pro adds tick/depth fields. Historical availability follows settlement. Package and date completeness, quote continuity, amendment/reissued-file handling and matching to an official off require an entitled audit. An exchange price is distinct from a Sky Bet price. [Stream specification](https://github.com/betfair/stream-api-sample-code/blob/master/ESASwaggerSchema.json) · [Package fields](https://support.developer.betfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002407812-What-data-packages-are-available) · [Release lag](https://support.developer.betfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002427051-How-frequently-is-the-historical-data-updated) |

## Admission evidence to obtain

1. Document the rights holder, product and tier, jurisdictions, purposes, raw retention and deletion periods, permitted copies and reviewer access, attribution and derived/public export. Keep raw licensed bytes in an authorised store.
2. Reconcile meetings, races, runners and market IDs to a dated denominator. Namespace all provider IDs. Join by verified course, racing date and scheduled start plus native keys. Keep scheduled off, actual off, provider publication, receipt and attestation as distinct events. Quarantine ambiguous meetings, renamings, reschedules, dead heats, unmatched withdrawals and abandoned races.
3. Verify two result sources with compatible final fields and outcomes, plus a documented upstream and editorial lineage check. A hash shows unchanged bytes. Retain authorised raw versions, original observation time, reviewer and amendment-parent identity. Leave unresolved forks outside the scored evaluation.
4. For historical exchange comparison, require a pinned WIN market with all scoped runners, active prices and captured suspension/in-play transitions. Compare receipt versions and document missing markets, conflation, reissued files and sources of time. Keep retrospective market diagnostics separate from prospective observations.
