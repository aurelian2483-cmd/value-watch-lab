## Scientific result and reproducibility

[Measurement sensitivity](measurement-sensitivity.json) extends the earlier identification bounds by combining independent measurement error and a bounded missing-group mean shift. With coverage 70/120, measured observed mean −16 milli-units and artificial support [−120,120], let the observed true mean range be `[oL,oU]` under measurement error `eps`. If the missing true mean differs by at most `d`, the exact full mean interval is `p*oL + (1-p)*max(-120,oL-d)` through `p*oU + (1-p)*min(120,oU+d)`. Here `p=70/120`. Exhaustive rational enumeration matched all 15 analytic scenarios. At shift 30, the upper endpoint is −3.5 without measurement error and +0.5 with ±4 measurement error. This is a synthetic sign change across conditional identification sets, not a confidence interval or model result. The existing locked endpoint was never used.
