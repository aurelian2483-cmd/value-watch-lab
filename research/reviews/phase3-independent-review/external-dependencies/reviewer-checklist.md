# Independent reviewer checklist

The review target is draft PR 143 at `e03ff8668330a4c2020f4ccc364f935e38741db8`. The external probe is separately pinned in the [evidence index](README.md). This checklist is for an independent human review and contains no approvals.

| Check | Evidence to inspect and decision to record |
| --- | --- |
| **Reviewer independence** | Record reviewer identity, relevant expertise, affiliation, scope, conflicts, dates and which immutable versions they received. Confirm that the reviewer did not design, tune, operate or select the evaluated model; disclose ties to the project, providers or related betting interests. |
| **Registered question and population** | Match the registration SHA and exact A/B definitions to the frozen protocol. Verify the first 500 reviewed consecutive race pairs and minimum 60 Europe/London dates with complete final date handling and 180-day horizon. Former archives and outcome-informed selections stay outside the prospective sample. Report insufficient evidence if the locked endpoint is short. |
| **Statistical reliability** | Reproduce race-equal B-minus-A log loss from raw frozen paired vectors and the registered 20,000 race-date draws, seed 20261115. Check the denominator, unequal dates, reproducibility, Europe/London day boundaries and treatment of single-date samples. Confirm no optional interim stopping or method selection based on protected results. |
| **Dependence and missingness** | Inspect dates, meetings and repeated horses, interval coverage limitations, calendar gaps, unequal race counts, blackout periods and informative missingness. Reproduce the existing synthetic independent/AR(1), block, HAC and missingness findings. Quantify inclusion and outage denominators, stage-by-stage attrition and known data revisions. Keep sensitivity analyses descriptive and the registered endpoint fixed. |
| **Transactional integrity** | Reproduce the prior three defects and the disposable acceptance receipts. Inspect cross-process duplicate/conflict handling, per-record cutoff and freshness, audit/forecast atomicity, restores, corruption checks/restart, immutable amendments and retention limits. Inspect actual infrastructure evidence only within approved scope. |
| **Timestamp qualification** | Verify each receipt against approved pinned root, chain, policy OID, TSA signing EKU, UTC generation, SHA-256 digest of the exact pair bytes and original query nonce. Use the signed error bound and enforce strict `signed generation UTC + signed accuracy < scheduled start UTC − 5 minutes`. Validate distributed serial/revocation information including compromise, dated status access for each applicable CA, and retention/reverification. Distinguish transport latency and TLS trust from cryptographic proof and trustworthy time. Hosted synthetic samples are outside the registered ledger. |
| **Result provenance and completeness** | Verify authorised original bodies, IDs, eligible full fields, actual versus scheduled timing, revision lineage, and independent sources' acquisition or adjudication per race. Inspect omissions, ambiguous identities, retired/withdrawn records and retention agreements. Two copies of the official return count as one lineage. Preserve exact revisions and prohibit outcome-informed replacements. |
| **Reproducibility and custody** | Pin commits/manifests, independently regenerate protected forecast digests and acceptance reports using the commands in the prior handover, and check this package's SHA-256 file. Reproduce public synthetic receipt binding offline. Record OS/tool versions and deviations. Where raw transfer is barred, obtain matching provider access or report the item unverified. |
| **Conclusions and approvals** | Give a disposition and evidence pointer for each gate, with residual limitations and action owner. Separate retrospective experiments, fixture coverage and prospectively enrolled evidence. Record independent statistical and technical sign-off explicitly. No result here supports promotion or betting eligibility. |

## Suitable reviewers

- An independent applied statistician, biostatistician or econometrician with clustered forecast evaluation, temporal dependence, uncertainty coverage, missing-data analysis and preregistered designs.
- An independent PKI and evidence engineering practitioner familiar with CMS, RFC 3161, X.509 trust, certificate-status and long-term verification, supported by a reliability engineer with SQLite/file durability and audit recovery experience if needed.
- An independent racing-data steward, research-data librarian or data licensing specialist with source-rights and supplier lineage experience. A supplier may attest its own product fields and licence. Those attestations and audit of the research design are separate.

The [original synthetic study](../README.md), [transactional repairs](../transactional-repair/README.md), [evidence qualification](../evidence-qualification/README.md) and [protected-hash handover](../final-qualification/README.md) remain the reference packages. Reviewers should record each check as passed with evidence, failed with a reproducible example, or unresolved with the exact missing evidence. No reviewer has been engaged or signed off.
