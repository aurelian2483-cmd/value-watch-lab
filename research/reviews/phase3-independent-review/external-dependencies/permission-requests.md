## British official results and declaration rights

**Route** · BHA data and regulatory enquiries or Weatherbys Data Supply. Send the declaration paragraph separately to RDC or its authorised on-seller if required. Route race-day delivery questions to RMG/PA or the applicable venue rights holder. [BHA contacts](https://www.britishhorseracing.com/about/contact-bha/) · [Weatherbys Data Supply](https://weatherbys.co.uk/commercial/data-supply) · [RDC licensing](https://www.racecoursedatacompany.com/rate-card/) · [RMG licensing form](https://racecoursemediagroup.com/structure-area/race-day-data/)

**Subject** · Permission enquiry for a small reproducible British racing research sample

Hello,

I'm working on an isolated scientific comparison of horse-racing forecast methods and would like to establish whether we can use a small sample of licensed data for independent review. Could you offer a no-charge sample covering one agreed consecutive seven-day window, plus a documented example of an amended result if the sample contains none?

We are looking for the full population of scheduled races for the agreed coverage, including voids and abandonments, complete declared and final fields, withdrawals and all result states. We would like the original and each corrected official result, the authority or adjudicator responsible, native meeting/race/runner identifiers, version and parent identifiers, and scheduled off, actual off and release timestamps with timezone and clock semantics. A coverage or outage manifest would help establish which records should exist. For declarations, we need the original and updated versions with the withdrawals and their release times. Please confirm who controls the rights for each component and whether different distributions use the same underlying return.

Would you authorise machine delivery and private retention of the specific original bytes, previous versions, hashes and approved backups? Please specify the allowed research use, review and storage locations, retention/deletion periods, applicable restrictions and whether a named independent reviewer can access a private copy or independently obtain the same versions. We would also like permission to publish aggregate scientific metrics and non-reconstructable provenance information with agreed attribution. We will not put licensed raw data in a public repository.

If an isolated no-charge pilot or a no-charge ongoing research agreement is available, please give its permitted scope and conditions. If access requires a paid agreement, could you share the scope and an indicative quote for information only? This enquiry is not an order.

Kind regards,

Aurelian Borkowski

**Subject** · RFC 3161 profile, revocation and archival verification for scientific research

Hello,

We are evaluating RFC 3161 for proving that the exact bytes of scientific forecast records existed before a strict cutoff. We send only a SHA-256 imprint and a nonce. Could you confirm the authorised research endpoint and access terms, the policy OID, how we can confirm the root fingerprint independently, and the supported signing certificate chain and rollover process?

We need a signed accuracy field in the token, traceability to UTC including how clock errors or outages suppress issuance, and signed revocation information for the signer and each applicable issuing CA. Please specify the revocation mechanism, frequency, evidence available for key compromise and retention after expiry, and any permitted way to download and archive revocation records for external review. Please also confirm whether we may retain and provide the query, token, chain and revocation evidence to an independent reviewer and disclose aggregate reliability measurements.

If the public profile does not meet these requirements, is there a permitted no-cost research profile? If a standalone paid product is the only route, please provide documentation and an indicative quote only.
