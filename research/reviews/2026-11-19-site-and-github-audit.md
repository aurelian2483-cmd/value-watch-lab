## Sport-specific data leads

- **Tennis.** The [Sackmann mirror](https://github.com/micahp/tennis-point-by-point-archive) provides ATP/WTA history and Slam point-by-point data under the stated CC BY-NC-SA licence. The historical data is a surface-rating research candidate. The pending-event queue still needs contemporary results and correct treatment of retirements, walkovers and aliases.
- **Darts.** The live snapshot contains 80 display results and one timed provider fixture. D1's aggregate model has zero fixture assessments in the published analysis. [Wharton Darts Moneyball](https://github.com/WhartonSABI/mba2026_darts) provides detailed 2019–2025 Players Championship data, with separate permission considerations for the underlying archive. It can inform backtest design and still needs a separate current-event identity source.
- **Rugby Union and League.** Union has 19 sampled provider rows. League has the official ten-fixture calendar and a retrospective NRL replay. The [GitHub reuse register](../GITHUB_REUSE.md) documents `nrlR` and the UK result-table mismatch. Use competition-aware parsing and keep NRL, UK League and Union benchmarks separate.
- **Football, NFL and model evaluation.** The expanded football snapshot contains 212 discovered fixtures and 184 experimental forecasts. NFL already has a fixed Elo and the Wildcard already consumes the current research inputs. Reuse the project's existing log loss and Brier scoring first. [scikit-learn](https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn) can provide an independent score or calibration reference once comparable pairs exist.

This audit changes no active models, feeds or website routes. Model status stays **UNVALIDATED** and betting publication **BLOCKED**.
