# Prospective evidence boundary and adversarial review

The pinned shadow report regenerates **40 dashboard forecast observations and
seven archived paper projections**, all ineligible. The 608 earlier-date
reconstructions, 66 expanded finals, 18 legacy history rows and 734
single-source observations keep their separate tiers. The 51 candidate result
links and four legacy repeats are provenance diagnostics. None supplies a
canonical outcome receipt. Eligible real forecasts and outcomes remain zero.

The existing prospective tests withhold same-day and post-cutoff inputs,
missing or at-kickoff freeze attestations, duplicate fixtures, ambiguous ordered
teams, unsupported origins and mismatched outcome/conflicting receipt sets.
Lineage tests reject an apparently known model name with missing immutable
configuration evidence. Binding a hash to an archived row does not prove when
its inputs were first available.

The adversarial test in this phase reproduced one evaluator defect. A training
receipt could claim an earlier timestamp while carrying the exact payload digest
of an evaluation outcome. A re-sealed synthetic packet with a trusted test-only
pin previously reached scoring. Evaluation now withholds any forecast whose
declared input or market provenance shares an exact digest with a supplied
evaluation outcome receipt. The guard does not edit model inputs, formulas or
weights. It detects exact payload reuse within the submitted packet. Distinct
hashes still require independent chronology and acquisition review.
