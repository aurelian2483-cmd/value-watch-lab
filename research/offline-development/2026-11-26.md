## NFL development

Source: FiveThirtyEight, NFL game data at https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/nfl-elo-game/blob/fbec1afa38ece5befe24fb21be8ddba8eb160fe6/data/nfl_games.csv. Project describes its intended forecasting experiments in the accompanying README and carries the ABC News Internet Ventures 2021 licence. Only season, playoff indicator, date, teams, neutral site and points enter the replay. Recorded Elo fields are ignored. No source rows republished. NFLverse was considered but the underlying schedules repository's rights chain was unresolved for broader reuse.

All 2012–20 regular seasons reconciled to 256 games per season and 16 per team. 2012–16 warm-up, 2017–20 development. Equal-start team Elo with 55-point home adjustment (0 for neutral), K=20, 25% between-season regression and a common smoothed tie frequency with 1 tie in 200 pseudo-games. Ratings incorporate earlier game dates after a two-day delay. Actual ties update Elo by half a win. Source franchise IDs are used as provided. Three alternatives were fixed: zero home advantage, margin-scaled Elo updates (capped at 2.5 times the standard update), and an equal pool of the margin and standard forecasts. All scored 1,024 identical games. The margin model improved mean log loss and Brier in all four years. Top-label calibration error increased in 2017 and 2020. First local execution stopped on a sparse-checkout missing the shared scorer. Restoring the committed dependency resolved it. No parameters changed.

Both sports report per-season log loss, paired relative gains, Brier and calibration. Bootstrap intervals resample 20,000 sets of calendar weeks, preserving pairing and unequal cluster sizes. Within each sport, two-sided tails are Bonferroni-adjusted for three candidates by four seasons. Weeks are treated as exchangeable; between-week dependencies, historical search multiplicity and transport to other seasons remain unresolved. No football thresholds applied to other sports.
