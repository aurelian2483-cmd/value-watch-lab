# Prospective football shadow-capture protocol

Paper-only research. Models **UNVALIDATED**. Publication **BLOCKED**.
This is a new protocol for future captures. It imports no forecasts,
outcomes, prices or scores from the retrospective replay.

Track three fixed paths in parallel when their code, configuration and
complete pre-event runtime inputs can be reproduced. These are the standalone
baseline, standalone plus earlier-five form at alpha 0.025, and the expanded
current-season formula. Each retains its original denominators and clamp
order. The registry identifies exact archived source hashes and configurations.
No model, weight or threshold is selected from historical results.

Require a namespaced provider event ID, exact ordered participants, explicit
competition and season, and zoned kickoff. Every path must bind the same fixture
and complete input bytes with explicit receipt provenance. Observation,
creation, freeze, independent proof and record acceptance must precede kickoff.
Use the earliest independently verified schedule time. Withhold reschedules or
identity conflicts for review. Never fill missing inputs from historical archives.
Capture all three individually sealed records atomically, or withhold the fixture.

Append schedules, captures, reviewed outcome sets, supersessions and exclusions
to a separate research journal. Preserve prior bytes. Independently adjudicated
regulation-time finals require exact matching, receipt provenance, distinct
provider and acquisition lineages and an explicit review of the complete set.
Conflicts, missing review, postponements, abandonments and voids remain outside
eligible outcomes. Unknown or changed identity stops the fixture.

A later evaluation must use one common, predeclared fixture cohort across all
three paths. Use natural log loss with the evaluator's 1e-15 floor and unscaled
three-class Brier. Do not score before the agreed cohort window closes and all
outcomes are reviewed or explicitly excluded. Stop intake on missing lineage,
unsound time proof, inconsistent evidence or an inability to enforce
immutability. Cohort dates, attested source and reviewer identities, and external
paper archive promotion require independent approval before real intake can open.

This milestone rehearses the contract with synthetic fixtures only. It defines no
real collection schedule, independently verified timestamp, or prospective metric.
