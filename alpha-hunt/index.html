Independent research · Separate from Value Watch and Bottleneck Lab

Is an apparent edge real or an illusion?

Alpha Hunt is a proposed Bayesian edge-validation framework. Its central research question is whether evidence can estimate the probability that an apparent betting edge is genuine before capital is put at risk.

01 / Research objective

Validate evidence, not simply predict winners

The supplied handover explicitly states that Alpha Hunt is not itself a betting model. Its purpose is to investigate whether apparent differences between models and market prices can be distinguished from false signals.

A

Market–model disagreement

Investigate the gap between a model's estimates and a price-implied probability. Disagreement is a research input, not proof of value.

B

False Edge Laboratory

Study possible causes of misleading edges: model error, market information advantage, regime shifts and noise.

C

Posterior Edge Probability

Investigate whether calibration, reliability and other evidence can support a defensible posterior estimate.

Audit Issue 3 / Architecture v5 · Approved as research design

Research workflow

Alpha Hunt v5 is the agreed research architecture. The stages below are proposed design components, not evidence that the system has been implemented or validated. Earlier ACME and Zero-Based Reconstruction designs are retained as historical versions, not competing current architectures.

  1. 01

    Market data

    Capture prices and their source, timing and market definitions.

  2. 02

    Prediction model

    Obtain an independently defensible forecast as an input.

  3. 03

    Calibration

    Measure how reliable forecasts have been on historical data.

  4. 04

    Disagreement detection

    Record model-versus-market differences without treating them as verified edges.

  5. 05

    False Edge Laboratory

    Investigate the causes and frequency of misleading apparent edges.

  6. 06

    Bayesian posterior engine

    Proposed estimation component; the underlying probability model remains unvalidated.

  7. 07

    The proposed output, P(Real Edge | Evidence); not a calculated or proven value on this site.

  8. 08

    Decision layer

    Proposed only: thresholds and operational validation are not agreed.

  9. 09

    Not active; do not interpret the proposed design as authorisation to wager.

03 / Evidence and uncertainty

What the research does not establish yet

Simulation results are not real-market results

The handover describes a proposed 100,000-opportunity synthetic framework and explicitly acknowledges that its favourable synthetic data uses hidden truth to generate reliability, calibration and trust features. This may create misleading performance. No independent real-market success is claimed here.

PEP assumptions remain to be tested

The 5% real-edge prior is an assumption, not an empirically established frequency. The handover also lists alternative feature sets; a single final feature contract is not yet agreed.

Acceptance and rejection criteria need measurement

Proposed evaluation includes Brier score, log loss, ROC-AUC, decile analysis, ROI and Sharpe Ratio. No reproduced measured figures, deployment evidence or approved decision thresholds are supplied.

No claimed operational capability

This page does not run a predictor, calculate a PEP for a fixture, receive live prices, share data with Value Watch or place bets. Those capabilities would require separate implementation, evidence and approval.

Audit Issue 2 / Agreed project responsibilities

Three distinct responsibilities

The responsibilities below were agreed during the project audit. They define project purpose, not completed implementation or validated performance.

VW

Value Watch

Develop mathematical methods for predicting sports outcomes. Existing results, odds and models remain under Value Watch's control.

BL

Bottleneck Lab

Investigate weaknesses in those methods and research possible improvements. An identified weakness does not itself prove a new prediction works.

AH

Alpha Hunt

Independently investigate whether apparent betting advantages are genuine using read-only Value Watch evidence. The data connection and posterior model are not yet implemented or validated.
