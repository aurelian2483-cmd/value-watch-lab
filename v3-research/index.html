Top four ≠ paid fourth place

Top-four ranking coverage is a descriptive model metric. An each-way fourth-place payment depends on the observed bookmaker offer, actual starters, any promotion, non-runners and final settlement. Standard four-place terms for handicaps with at least 16 starters do not establish an offer for this retrospective sample. We have no validated place probabilities or betting EV.
