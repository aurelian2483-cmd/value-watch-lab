Validation release gates

  1. Freeze each forecast before its event begins, with a unique prediction ID and source snapshot fingerprint.
  2. Confirm a final outcome with matching event and market identity and independent source evidence.
  3. Verify chronology, duplicates, contradictory results and leakage risks before numerical scoring.
  4. Assess sample adequacy, calibration, Brier/log loss, uncertainty and chronological independent baselines per sport.
  5. Evaluate genuine bookmaker prices and settlement rules separately; no model promotion or positive-EV claims without approval.

This dashboard never edits the research models, betting slips, archived predictions, or result collectors. It cannot itself attest that external provider receipts are genuine.
