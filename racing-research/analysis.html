BOG and level-stakes discipline

Best Odds Guaranteed

Check the named bookmaker’s eligibility, time restrictions, market and account terms. Record the taken price and later SP separately. BOG is not assumed and does not make an otherwise poor price valuable.

1pt WIN / 0.5pt each-way

Compare systems at fixed stakes. A 0.5pt each-way bet costs 1pt in total. Each-way evaluation needs place probability, place fraction, number of places and exact settlement terms; the win calculator does not price it.

Settlement before profit

Check non-runners, Rule 4, dead heats, extra places, voids and exchange commission. No progressive staking, Martingale or loss-chasing. No ROI is calculated from missing or hypothetical prices.

Record BOG eligibility, taken price, SP and each-way terms in the linked notes below. This worksheet does not recommend stakes or place bets.
