Useful checks for horse-racing advice

Price sensitivity

A tip at 6/1 is a different proposition at 3/1. Keep publication time, the quoted price and the price actually obtainable. A shortening price alone does not prove value.

Course, trip and going

Check the relevant surface and distance, pace setup, current going and non-runners. A course winner on different conditions may offer little comparable evidence.

Hidden form

Ask for a replay or stewards’ report before accepting claims about interference, a missed break, a wide trip or an unsuitable pace. Record the counterargument too.

Class, weight and connections

Read class and handicap changes in context. Trainer or jockey names are not performance statistics; test records on suitable, dated samples.

Source overlap

Two posters can repeat one column. Shared wording or attribution is an overlap flag. Two publishers are not automatically two independent opinions.

Review the full record

Keep losing, winning and void selections. Compare fixed stakes, identical periods, obtainable prices and each-way terms before ranking a tipster.

Source coverage and research roles

Rob Wright, Hugh Taylor, Pricewise and Paul Kealy remain possible research inputs. TRF, Fat Jockey, the UK Betting Forum, Punters Lounge, OLBG and r/HorseRacingUK need post-level evidence. No audited “best tipster” ranking is established here.
