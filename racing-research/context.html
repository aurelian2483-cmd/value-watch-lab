What this model does and does not know

The calculation uses comparable dated finishing evidence and records how much past form matches the current trip, going, course and class. The weights are exploratory choices, not fitted winning probabilities.

Coverage counts expose missing evidence. There is no invented draw bias, pace map, trainer/jockey advantage, replay judgement, fitness assessment or live rail-movement analysis. A bookmaker favourite and a context-model leader may differ.

Recorded price moves describe earlier source quotes. Shortening prices and agreement between commentators do not, by themselves, prove value.
